Privacy policy

For EA, a personal assistant used only by its owner. About EA

Who uses it

EA is used by one person, its owner, on their own computer. It has no other users, no accounts and no server.

What it reads from Google

EA asks Google for one permission only: to read the owner's Gmail (gmail.readonly). It reads the email threads the owner has labelled for it. It cannot send, delete, label or change any email, and it reads no other Google data.

Where it is kept

  • Email that EA reads is saved on the owner's computer, in a folder only the owner's account can open. It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • The Google sign-in is kept in the computer's own password store (the macOS Keychain).

What is shared

  • EA does not sell, share or publish Google data, and does not use it for advertising.
  • The owner may choose to have some messages translated and summarized by an AI service (Anthropic's Claude). If so, each message is shown to the owner and sent only once the owner approves it. Email addresses, links and phone numbers are removed first. Messages the owner marks as restricted, and messages with sensitive words or numbers, are never sent.
  • EA does not use Google data to train AI models.

Removing access and data

  • The owner can withdraw EA's access at any time from their Google Account, under Third-party apps and services, or from EA itself.
  • Deleting EA's folder on the owner's computer deletes everything it saved.

Google's rules

EA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
