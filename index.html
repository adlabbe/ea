EA

A personal assistant for one volunteer member of a condominium board.

EA runs on its owner's own computer and is used by its owner alone. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

It brings the board's work into one list: email that needs a reply, votes and payments waiting for approval, meeting action items, and dates that are coming up.

What it does with Gmail

With its owner's permission, EA reads the owner's Gmail, read-only, so that board email shows up in that list. It cannot send, delete, label or change any email. Everything it reads stays on the owner's computer.
